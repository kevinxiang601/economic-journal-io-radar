# NBER and RSS discovery

Preserve these channels alongside the journal workflow.

## NBER

Read the current NBER-CLI instructions at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SepineTam/nber-cli/master/plugins/nber-cli/skills/NBER-CLI/SKILL.md before using that CLI. Fetch the entire current feed with `uvx nber-cli feed fetch --display-all true --format json`, without a max-items cutoff. Inspect the actual output schema (`results` or `items`) rather than assuming it. Verify retained records using `uvx nber-cli info PAPER_ID --all --refresh --format json` and NBER's official paper pages and New This Week inventory. If the CLI is unavailable, use the official inventories and disclose the fallback.

Use Asia/Shanghai dates: first publications in the current calendar week (Monday through run date), extending at most to the immediately preceding Monday–Sunday week. A Monday-morning run may precede the new US release; state this and inspect the previous week. Use official dated search or historical weekly inventories, following pagination when the feed omits the preceding week. Do not confuse revision dates, RSS timestamps, or cache first-seen dates with first release.

NBER is a selective research feed, not a broad economics digest. Use the same [v2 scoring and gates](scoring.md): topic35/method45/anchor10/extension10. Prioritize game theory, structural IO, and credible DID/RD(RDD)/DDD/IV/RCT; neither NBER affiliation nor a technique name is an admission reason. Evaluate the entire feed for recall, then screen for one of two evidence-based admission routes:

1. **Direct topic**: IO, platforms, algorithmic competition, substantive AI intersections, or directly relevant demand/marketing/firm-strategy research. The research question and actual mechanism must match, not just the title or an industry label.
2. **Method or perspective contribution**: another field may qualify if the paper itself provides a concrete, useful IO/game-theoretic/consumer-demand mechanism, modeling insight, identification design, or perspective. Explain (a) the specific result or design actually supported by the paper, (b) what changes in how one analyzes incentives, competition, information, pricing, demand, or consumer choice, and (c) one concrete research use and its conditions. No fully developed new project is required. This route does not demand an explicit platform/AI label or a formal game model in every paper.

Favor four/five-star NBER entries. A three-star entry can remain in the main list only when its specific method/perspective contribution is demonstrated, not merely claimed as potentially transferable. Add one concise `- 入选依据：直接议题｜...` or `- 入选依据：方法或视角贡献｜...` field before the abstract; for three stars use the contribution route and state the actual insight/design and concrete use. Keep publisher sources as the final field. Do not inflate scores to keep a candidate. Weak links and generic tool borrowing are excluded rather than accumulated in observation; keep an observation only if it offers a specific defensible research lead.

Calibration example from the user's feedback: food assistance research may be retained for distinguishing the composition of purchases from total expenditure after income support. This can inform demand modeling and category substitution even though its original policy is not a platform policy. Judge the evidence and the contribution, not the policy-domain label. Such a result can justify a selective three-star entry; it does not automatically prove firm competition, strategic interaction, mental accounting, or a novel demand-estimation method, and must not be described as doing so without evidence. Conversely, shipping bottlenecks, monetary price adjustment, licensing, or health policies do not qualify merely because one could imagine replacing their participants with firms or platform sellers. They still may qualify if their actual method or perspective makes the specified substantive contribution, subject to the transport/optimization caps below.

Environmental intersections remain secondary, requiring topic>=31/35, method>=28/45 and final>=70; mark them `（环境）`. Exclude environmental one- to three-star papers from main/observation lists, with aggregate audit counts only. Standalone environmental inclusion remains reserved for the Top 5 journal track. Neither admission route overrides this environmental threshold or the transport/optimization exclusions.

Within that scope, prioritize IO, platforms, algorithmic competition, and substantive AI intersections. Supply-chain/network research lacking a core IO or AI mechanism has topic<=23 and at most three stars, retained only for concrete transferable value; incidental network macro papers usually drop out. Optimization/transport-led papers have topic<=17 and at most two stars, normally excluded or rare low-relevance observation. AI-based routing, forecasting, scheduling or optimization does not qualify as AI×IO without an actual strategic/market mechanism. These restrictions apply equally to NBER and journal papers.

Place `## NBER Working Papers（本周／上周新发布）` after all journal sections and before `## 2星观察区`, even for zero additions. Clearly label working-paper status; supply the NBER number, first release date, official link, and DOI or `DOI暂缺`. Preserve the full source-order Chinese abstract, three-sentence explanation, and sources last. Add NBER coverage counts to the existing audit.

Deduplicate all prior main lists, observation sections, cumulative lists, configured research digests, and existing paper filenames; optionally read Zotero only when needed. Match NBER ID and DOI, then normalized title/author combinations, and inspect published-version links or author pages for renamed versions. Different DOIs do not make different studies. Prefer a verified journal version when discovered simultaneously. A previously introduced working paper or published version is not a new entry; record the version link in the audit/cumulative record. Unintroduced pending candidates can be included once verified. CLI cache state is never the historical inclusion ledger.

## Publisher RSS

Discover official Latest Issue and Advance/Early View/In Press feed URLs from publisher subscription links; validate XML, journal identity, entries, and dates. Do not invent URLs or substitute research-highlight/citation alerts for journal inventories. Union feed results with official TOCs and registries, and investigate differences. Publisher RSS and the publisher website are the same source, not two independent channels. Unavailable, delayed, or short feeds require fallback to the normal workflow and disclosure in the audit, not a zero-paper conclusion. Follow the same rules for ReStud. Crossref discovery must consider both DOI creation and online-publication dates.

Record validated checkpoints in the task's continuation notes if a run is interrupted. Resume verified work instead of repeating searches and downloads. Never publish these notes with the skill.
